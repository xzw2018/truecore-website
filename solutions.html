01 / OFFICE NETWORK

Office switching
and wireless access.

Select access switches, uplinks and cabling for workstations, wireless access points and shared devices. Plan port capacity, VLANs and PoE before adding equipment.

  • Count existing devices and access points, with room for expansion.
  • Check PoE standards, per-port power and the total power budget.
  • Specify uplink speeds, cable distances and management features.

02 / COMPUTE INFRASTRUCTURE

Servers and
data center switching.

Size servers for application, virtualization and storage workloads, then match network adapters and switches to the expected traffic. Check rack space, power and cooling as part of the hardware plan.

  • Size CPU, memory and storage for applications and virtual machines.
  • Match NICs, switch ports and optics to the planned link speeds.
  • Verify rack dimensions, power capacity and cooling.

03 / UPGRADES & SPARE PARTS

Hardware upgrades
and replacement parts.

Add memory, drives, power supplies or network components to an existing system. Start with exact model and part numbers, then check supported configurations and plan the replacement or upgrade.

  • Record system models and the part numbers being added or replaced.
  • Verify slot availability, protocols, power and firmware support.
  • Prepare backups, a maintenance window and a rollback plan.
